# Itachi's darkest sacrifice for Sasuke (40 s, approved)

1. Imagine giving up your name, your home, and your brother's love, just to keep him alive.
2. At just 13, Itachi Uchiha learned his clan was planning a coup against their own village.
3. To stop a war, he was ordered to wipe out the entire clan in one night.
4. He did it, but he spared one boy: his little brother, Sasuke.
5. Then he let Sasuke hate him, so that hate would make him strong.
6. For eight years he lived as a criminal, secretly spying to protect the village.
7. Sick and dying, he staged one final fight and hid a protective power in Sasuke's eye.
8. He died at 21 with a gentle tap on his brother's forehead. Sometimes the deepest love looks exactly like betrayal.

All visuals are original: the brothers are two flame-spirits (big cyan = the elder, small yellow = the younger),
the clan is a row of lanterns, the narrator is Pip (a round violet pebble with a yellow scarf). No anime likenesses, no red.
